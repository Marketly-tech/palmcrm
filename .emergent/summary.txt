<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
Build a web-based POST-SALES Internal CRM for a real estate developer called RRL Builders. The user provided a reference website (https://rrlbuildersanddevelopers.com/) and a Figma board (https://www.figma.com/board/4qh3OuL9l8dhFU2K6fmvdR/Marketlu?node-id=23-17) for UI inspiration.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
The CRM must manage the entire post-booking lifecycle of a real estate customer, replacing Excel sheets and automating various processes.

- **Module 1 (Booking Form Integration):** Integrate with Google Forms to create new customer records automatically and send a welcome email.
- **Module 2 (Customer Profile):** Detailed customer profiles with sections for personal details, payment schedule, documents, and communication history.
- **Module 3 (Price Breakup Calculator):** An automated calculator for total property price based on various inputs like area, rates, and charges.
- **Module 4 (Payment Plan):** A module to create and track payment schedules with milestones, due dates, and statuses. A dashboard view for pending/overdue payments.
- **Module 5 (Document Generation):** Create editable templates for Sales Agreements, Allotment Letters, etc., with dynamic fields, downloadable as PDFs.
- **Module 6 (Agreement Management):** A workflow to manage agreements from draft to completion.
- **Module 7 (Email Automation):** Integrate email (SendGrid was chosen) for automated communications like welcome emails, payment reminders, and document sharing.
- **Module 8 (WhatsApp Reminders):** Integrate WhatsApp (Twilio was chosen) for automated reminders.
- **Module 9 (Document Checklist):** A checklist for each customer to track received documents (KYC, PAN, etc.).
- **Module 10 (Dashboard):** A main dashboard with KPIs (total customers, pending agreements, payments due) and charts for admins (revenue, payment status).
- **Module 11 (User Roles):** Implement roles: Admin, Manager, Accounts, Sales Team, Customer Support.
- **Security:** Role-based access control, secure document storage, and activity logs.
